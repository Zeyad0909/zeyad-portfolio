About

A mathematician's habits, applied to software.

I'm a Mathematics student at Helwan University, and I've completed the ITI training in Web Development Using Python. That training is where my interest in software turned into practical work: I studied HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, Django, PostgreSQL, SQL fundamentals and Bootstrap, and used them to build applications.

Mathematics taught me to approach a problem logically and to split a large task into smaller steps I can solve one at a time. I bring the same approach to code, whether I'm designing database tables, tracing a bug or deciding how a feature should work from the user's side.

I'm interested in more than websites. I want to understand how a complete application fits together, from the interface to the database, and I learn best by building projects and finding out where my understanding has gaps. My goal is to become a well-rounded developer who keeps adding tools, languages and good practices over time.

  • Break problems down

    Large tasks become small, testable steps.

  • Learn by building

    Projects show me what I actually understand.

  • See the whole application

    Interface, logic and data all have to work together.
